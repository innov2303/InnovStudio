---
name: SEO agent recommendation scope
description: Keep automated SEO recommendations relevant, evidence-based, and applicable.
---

Do not recommend edits to the meta-keywords tag: major search engines such as Google do not use it for ranking, and the app cannot apply those edits. Do not insert weekly trend phrases into a page's metadata unless they clearly match that page's audience and service.

**Why:** the agent produced non-applicable keyword edits and mismatched phrases such as artisan/restaurant terms on an enterprise-application page.

**How to apply:** generate actions only for supported metadata fields and ensure suggestions stay faithful to the specific page and offer.
